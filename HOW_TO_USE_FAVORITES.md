# How to Use Favorites

## Adding Favorites

1. Open any adhkar category (Morning, Evening, or Night)
2. Swipe through the adhkar
3. When you find one you love, **tap the heart icon** in the top right
4. The heart will **fill with red** and you'll feel a haptic feedback
5. That's it! The adhkar is now in your favorites

## Viewing Favorites

1. Tap the **Favorites tab** at the bottom (heart icon)
2. See all your favorite adhkar organized by category:
   - 🌅 **Morning** - Adhkar Al-Sabah
   - 🌇 **Evening** - Adhkar Al-Masaa  
   - 🌙 **Night** - Before Sleep
3. Each category shows a count of how many favorites you have
4. Tap any adhkar to read it in full detail

## Removing Favorites

1. Open the adhkar you want to remove from favorites
2. Tap the **filled red heart** in the top right
3. The heart will become an outline (unfilled)
4. The adhkar is removed from your favorites

## Tips

- ⭐ **Quick Access:** Use favorites for adhkar you want to memorize
- 📚 **Personal Collection:** Build your own custom selection
- 🎯 **Focus:** When starting out, favorite essential adhkar
- 💫 **Organized:** All favorites are automatically grouped by time

## Automatic Level Progression

### Level Up (🎉 Promotion)
- Complete **all 3 adhkar** (morning, evening, night) daily
- Do this for **10 days in a row**
- You'll automatically move to the next level
- See a celebration modal!

### Level Down (⚠️ Adjustment)  
- Miss any adhkar for **2 days in a row**
- You'll automatically move down one level
- Don't worry - you can rebuild your streak!

### How It Works
- **Level 1 → 2:** 10 perfect days ✅
- **Level 2 → 3:** Another 10 perfect days ✅
- **Level 3 → 2:** Miss 2 days ❌
- **Level 2 → 1:** Miss 2 days ❌

### Perfect Day Requirements
A "perfect day" means completing:
- ✅ Morning adhkar (after Fajr)
- ✅ Evening adhkar (after Asr)
- ✅ Night adhkar (after Maghrib)

### Manual Control
- You can still manually change levels anytime
- Go to Settings → Level System
- Pick your preferred level
- Auto-progression will resume from there

## FAQ

**Q: Do favorites sync across devices?**  
A: Not yet - they're stored locally on your device.

**Q: Is there a limit to how many favorites I can have?**  
A: No limit! Add as many as you like.

**Q: Will I lose favorites if I uninstall the app?**  
A: Yes, favorites are stored locally. Consider noting them down if reinstalling.

**Q: Can I turn off automatic level progression?**  
A: Yes! Go to Settings → Level System → Toggle "Auto Progression" off.

**Q: What happens if I miss 1 day but complete the next?**  
A: Your consecutive missed days resets to 0. You need 2 days **in a row** to level down.

**Q: Can I favorite adhkar from different levels?**  
A: Yes! Favorites work independently of levels.
