# How to Use the Level System

## For Users

### Accessing Level Settings
1. Open the app
2. Go to the **Settings** tab (bottom right)
3. Tap on **Level System**

### Enabling the Level System
1. Toggle **Enable Level System** to ON
2. The level options will appear

### Choosing Your Level

#### 🍃 Level 1 - Beginner
**Best for:**
- New to adhkar practice
- Building initial habits
- Want to start with essentials

**What you get:**
- 9 essential adhkar for morning
- 9 essential adhkar for evening  
- 9 essential adhkar for night
- Core protection and remembrance

#### ⭐ Level 2 - Intermediate
**Best for:**
- Consistent with Level 1
- Ready to expand practice
- Building stronger habits

**What you get:**
- All Level 1 adhkar (9)
- Plus 4-5 additional adhkar per period
- Total: 13-14 adhkar per period
- More comprehensive protection

#### 🔥 Level 3 - Advanced
**Best for:**
- Established practice
- Committed to full sunnah
- Complete adhkar collection

**What you get:**
- All Level 1 & 2 adhkar (13-14)
- Plus 1-2 final adhkar per period
- Total: 15-16 adhkar per period
- Complete traditional collection

### Disabling the Level System

At any time, you can:
1. Go back to **Level System** settings
2. Toggle **Enable Level System** to OFF
3. All adhkar will be shown regardless of level

## Tips

### Starting Out
- Begin with Level 1 even if you're familiar with adhkar
- Focus on consistency rather than quantity
- Move up when you're comfortable

### Progressing
- Stay at each level for at least 2-4 weeks
- Ensure you can complete the adhkar consistently
- Only move up when ready

### Flexibility
- You can change levels anytime
- You can disable the system to see all adhkar
- The setting is saved across app restarts

## Example Daily Practice

### Level 1 (Beginner)
- **Morning:** 9 adhkar (~5-10 minutes)
- **Evening:** 9 adhkar (~5-10 minutes)
- **Night:** 9 adhkar (~5-10 minutes)
- **Total:** 27 adhkar/day

### Level 2 (Intermediate)
- **Morning:** 14 adhkar (~8-15 minutes)
- **Evening:** 13 adhkar (~8-15 minutes)
- **Night:** 14 adhkar (~8-15 minutes)
- **Total:** 41 adhkar/day

### Level 3 (Advanced)
- **Morning:** 16 adhkar (~10-20 minutes)
- **Evening:** 15 adhkar (~10-20 minutes)
- **Night:** 15 adhkar (~10-20 minutes)
- **Total:** 46 adhkar/day

## Benefits

✅ **Gradual Progression** - Build habits slowly and sustainably  
✅ **Less Overwhelming** - Start with what's manageable  
✅ **Motivation** - Clear path to advance  
✅ **Flexibility** - Can disable anytime to see all  
✅ **Authentic** - All adhkar from authentic sources
