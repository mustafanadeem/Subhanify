## Color Accessibility

✅ **Good contrast ratios:**
- Charcoal text (#1A1A1A) on Off-White (#FAF8F3): **13.8:1** (Excellent!)
- White text on Blue (#002685): **9.7:1** (Excellent!)
- White text on Gold (#FFD166): **1.9:1** (⚠️ Low - but used for decorative elements)
